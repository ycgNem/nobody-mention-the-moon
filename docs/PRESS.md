# Press kit (draft)

Text for an itch.io page or a store listing. Screenshots, GIFs and the trailer still need capturing from a real session with friends (M8).

## One-paragraph pitch

You and up to five friends are catering a dinner party, and you're the only humans in the house. Every guest is a monster in a bad disguise, and every one of them is convinced *you* are a legendary monster too. Cook the food, seat the guests, keep the small talk on safe ground, and keep your lies straight, because each one becomes a house rule your whole team has to live by. Talk with proximity voice chat, and be careful what you say near the table. The guests are listening, and saying "moon" near the werewolf is a very bad idea. If they work out what you are, there's no game-over screen. Run for the van.

## Fact sheet

- **Genre:** co-op party game, first person
- **Players:** 1–6, online. Solo works too.
- **Session:** one dinner takes about 10–15 minutes, and every dinner is generated fresh from a seed you can share
- **Platform:** desktop browser. Tested in Chrome and Edge; Firefox and Safari should work but are untested. No install, no account.
- **Voice:** proximity voice chat with room muffling. Trigger words are spotted on your own device; your audio never leaves it.
- **No progression:** no XP, unlocks or grind. Everything is available from the first dinner.

## Features

- **8 monsters in disguise:** werewolf, vampire, ghost, fae, shapeshifter, gorgon, mummy, zombie. Each has its own tells, triggers, appetite and quirks.
- **7 occasions:** dinner party, birthday, book club, wedding rehearsal, surprise party, ex reunion, and Hunter Night, where the entertainment is a Monster Hunter.
- **10 events:** the full moon rising, the neighbour dropping by, a power cut, a thunderstorm, the dog getting out, a wine spill, the pizza driver, a party game, a late guest, and a toast you have to give out loud.
- **Your lies become the rules:** tell the ghost you don't do mirrors either, and now nobody on your team can go near the bathroom.
- **3 houses:** a family house, a cramped city flat where the kitchen is in earshot of the table, and a country manor where it's a long walk to the wine.
- **A real kitchen:** chop, cook and plate every dish yourself, and find out what happens when the garlic bread reaches the wrong guest.
- **The Chase:** blow your cover and the whole table turns on you. Make it to the van.
